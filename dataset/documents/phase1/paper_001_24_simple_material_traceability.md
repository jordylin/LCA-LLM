## Executive Summary

This material traceability report documents the complete material flow for production lot SLM-BATCH-316L-033, comprising 33 units of 316L stainless steel flat washers manufactured via selective laser melting. The production cycle demonstrates a material utilization efficiency of 14.8% for finished parts, with significant material recovery through powder reuse and water recycling systems. All materials have been tracked from initial receipt through processing to final disposition, maintaining full chain of custody documentation.
