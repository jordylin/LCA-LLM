## Executive Summary

This report documents the quality inspection for the manufacturing of a single ER70 steel marine propeller produced via Selective Laser Melting (SLM). The inspection was conducted to verify conformance with specified material, dimensional, and process parameters. The propeller was built on an SLM Solutions 280 2.0 system using gas-atomized ER70 steel powder, followed by CNC finish machining to achieve final dimensions.

All critical-to-quality parameters were found to be within acceptable limits. The finished propeller mass was verified at 0.204 kg. Process consumables and energy usage were logged and aligned with expected operational ranges. The inspection concludes that the part conforms to all specified requirements and is approved for customer delivery.

***

## 1.0 Inspection Scope and Objectives

The scope of this inspection encompassed the complete manufacturing sequence for one (1) ER70 steel marine propeller, from raw material preparation through to final post-processing. The primary objectives were:

*   To verify the quality and specification of incoming steel powder feedstock.
*   To monitor and record key process parameters during the SLM build cycle.
*   To inspect the final part geometry, surface finish, and mass.
*   To document all material inputs, energy consumption, and waste outputs for traceability and process control.
*   To ensure full compliance with the technical data sheet and customer purchase order specifications.

The inspection was performed in accordance with our internal quality management system, which is aligned with the referenced ASTM and ISO standards.

***

## 2.0 Process Description

The manufacturing process for the subject propeller consists of three primary stages:

1.  **Powder Feedstock Production:** A certified ER70 steel billet was processed via inert gas atomization to produce the fine metal powder required for the SLM process. This sub-process consumes electricity, argon gas, and cooling water.
2.  **Selective Laser Melting (SLM):** The steel powder was deposited in thin layers within a build chamber filled with an argon atmosphere. A high-power laser selectively melted the powder according to the propeller's digital CAD model, building the part layer-by-layer. This stage included the simultaneous fabrication of support structures, which were removed after the build.
3.  **Finish Machining:** The as-built propeller underwent CNC machining to remove the 1 mm allowance from all functional surfaces, achieving the final dimensional tolerances and surface finish specified by the customer. This operation consumed electricity and cutting fluid.

***

## 3.0 Inspection Procedures and Methodology

A combination of measurement, monitoring, and visual inspection techniques was employed throughout the production process.

**3.1 Material and Feedstock Inspection**
*   **Method:** Certificate of Analysis (CoA) review for the steel billet; sieve analysis and Hall Flowmeter test for the produced powder.
*   **Standard:** Powder lot must conform to internal specification AM-MAT-ER70-01.

**3.2 In-Process Monitoring**
*   **Method:** Automated data logging from the SLM machine (power, gas flow, chamber atmosphere) and the CNC machine (power, fluid flow). All utilities are metered with calibrated instruments.
*   **Standard:** Process parameters must remain within the validated operating window defined in process work instruction WI-SLM-07.

**3.3 Final Product Inspection**
*   **Dimensional Check:** Performed using a coordinate measuring machine (CMM) with a probe accuracy of ±1.5 µm. Critical dimensions including pitch, chord length, and blade thickness were measured at 15 points per blade.
*   **Mass Measurement:** Weighed using a calibrated precision scale with an accuracy of ±0.001 kg.
*   **Visual Inspection:** Conducted under 10x magnification to check for surface defects such as cracks, porosity, and remnants of support structures.

**3.4 Resource and Waste Tracking**
*   All input materials (powder, gases, fluids) and output materials (part, waste) were weighed and recorded. Energy consumption was directly read from calibrated kWh meters on the respective equipment.

## 5.0 Conformance Assessment

Based on the data collected and inspections performed, the following conformance statements are made:

*   **Material Conformance:** The ER70 steel powder used for the build conforms to the chemical and physical requirements per its CoA and internal specification AM-MAT-ER70-01.
*   **Process Conformance:** All recorded SLM and machining parameters (laser power, scan speed, hatch spacing, feed rates) were maintained within the approved operational ranges defined in work instructions WI-SLM-07 and WI-CNC-04.
*   **Product Conformance:** The final marine propeller meets all dimensional, mass, and visual quality criteria as specified in drawing MP-ER70-SLM-001. The part is free from cracks, significant porosity, and other manufacturing defects that would affect its serviceability.

**Overall Conformance Status: PASS**
