## 2.0 Process Overview

The manufacturing process involves two primary stages: powder production via water atomization and component fabrication via Selective Laser Melting.

**Water Atomization:** Stainless steel feedstock is melted and atomized using high-pressure water jets to produce fine powder for SLM processing. Process water serves as both cooling and atomization media.

**Selective Laser Melting:** Metal powder is deposited in thin layers and selectively melted by a laser beam under inert argon atmosphere to build components layer-by-layer. Unused powder is recovered for reuse or recycling.
