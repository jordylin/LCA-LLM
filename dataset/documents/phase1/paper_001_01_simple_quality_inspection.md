# Quality Inspection Report: Selective Laser Melting Process for 316L Stainless Steel Flat Washers

**Report Number:** QIR-SLM-316L-2023-08-015  
**Date of Inspection:** August 15, 2023  
**Inspector:** Maria Chen, Senior Quality Engineer  
**Facility:** Advanced Manufacturing Division, Building C  
**Process Evaluated:** Selective Laser Melting (SLM) of 316L Stainless Steel Flat Washers  
**Batch Reference:** SLM-WSH-316L-230815-33

---

## 1.0 Executive Summary

This quality inspection report documents the verification of manufacturing processes and output quality for Batch SLM-WSH-316L-230815-33, comprising 33 units of 316L stainless steel flat washers produced via Selective Laser Melting. The inspection covered material handling, process parameters, energy utilization, and final product quality against internal specification AM-QS-316L-Rev4 and customer requirements.

All process parameters operated within established control limits. The manufactured washers demonstrated full compliance with dimensional and material specifications. Material utilization efficiency measured at 14.8% for finished product yield, with 71.5% of input powder successfully recovered for reuse. Energy consumption patterns aligned with equipment specifications and historical baselines.

**Overall Assessment:** PROCESS APPROVED - All quality parameters within acceptable limits.

## 8.0 Conclusions and Recommendations

### 8.1 Quality Compliance Statement

The Selective Laser Melting process for 316L stainless steel flat washers (Batch SLM-WSH-316L-230815-33) has been verified to operate within all specified quality parameters. The 33 produced washers conform to dimensional, material, and finish requirements. Material and energy consumption patterns are consistent with equipment capabilities and process optimization targets.

**Final Determination:** All inspection criteria satisfied. Batch approved for shipment.

### 8.2 Process Improvement Opportunities

1. **Powder Recovery Efficiency:** Current recovery rate of 71.5% presents opportunity for improvement. Recommendation: Evaluate sieve mesh optimization and handling procedures to target 75% recovery.

2. **Energy Consumption:** While within acceptable ranges, the 64.92 kWh consumption for SLM processing suggests potential for build layout optimization. Suggestion: Review nesting software parameters to reduce laser travel distance.

3. **Water Usage:** The 16.8 kg water consumption with 97.6% recovery rate demonstrates effective water management. Continue current practices.

### 8.3 Follow-up Actions

- Schedule preventive maintenance for powder recovery system (Due: September 30, 2023)
- Conduct calibration verification for power monitoring equipment (Due: August 30, 2023)
- Document lessons learned for future similar production runs
